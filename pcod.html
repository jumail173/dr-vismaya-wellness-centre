What is PCOD / PCOS?

Understanding the condition behind the label.

PCOD (Polycystic Ovarian Disease) and PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) are common hormonal conditions that affect women in their twenties, thirties and beyond. They occur when the ovaries produce slightly higher levels of male hormones (androgens), disrupting the delicate balance of female hormones.

This usually shows up as irregular periods, hormonal acne, unwanted facial hair, weight gain, and difficulty getting pregnant. It is also closely linked to thyroid function, insulin sensitivity and chronic stress — which is why the right treatment looks at the whole person, not just the ovaries.

Because homoeopathy treats each woman as an individual, Dr. Vismaya’s approach to PCOS combines a carefully selected constitutional remedy with gentle lifestyle guidance — aiming for regular cycles and steady hormonal balance that lasts beyond the course of treatment.
